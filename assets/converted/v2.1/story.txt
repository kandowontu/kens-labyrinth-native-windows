Entering Floor I, Volume I
"Once upon a time..."

You awake in a strange world.  One
intention is burned in your mind - to
escape from this place and save the
Earth from destruction.  It won't be
easy.

* To view the full story for this
episode, please look under the STORY 
menu.
\
Entering Floor II, Volume I 
"Bat Cave"

This floor is filled with bats.  They
camouflage very well, so look out.

* There is an extensive network of
secret passages in this floor.  All of
them are connected to each other - they
are filled with power-ups, so be on the
lookout!
\
Entering Floor III, Volume I
"Fence-Bound"

You may find that you are at a locked
door with no key; if so, backtrack
until you find it.

* This board is pretty straightforward;
you don't need any hints.
\
Entering Floor IV, Volume I
"Happy Hackers"

The warp zone is the only route to the
end of this floor.  Go through the warp
several times because it leads to many
different places.

* If you want to go on to the next room
that the warp leads to, just re-enter 
the warp.
\
Entering Floor V, Volume I
"Find Direction"

There are many secret passages in this
floor, most of them within other secret
passages.

* The only effective way to get rid of
8-balls is to have them fall down a
hole.  Also be sure to get the compass
because it is found only on this floor.
\
Entering Floor VI, Volume I
"Decisions, decisions"

At the end of the floor, there will
be a choice of 5 paths to take; shoot
the wrong one and you're monster meat.

* You will find a warp entrance in the
room of disks.  Your first bouncy
bullet is well hidden near a door.
\
Entering Floor VII, Volume I
"Aland"

If you see a ghost, take it as a sign
for secret passages nearby.  You will
find weapons to the left, and a
key to the right of the start.

* Don't warp too many times and be
sure to search the bat caves.
\
Entering Floor VIII, Volume I
"Out in the Open"

This new type of floor has no visible
outer wall - but if you stray too far
from the center of the maze, you will
be prevented from going further.

* Once you have the keys you will have
to backtrack to near your starting
point to find the exit.
\
Entering Floor IX, Volume I
"Passages"

You will find the gold key in the disk
room.  The locked silver door is near
the start of the floor.

* Be nice to those pictures of Ken,
because they could hurt you.
\
Entering Floor X, Volume I
"_____ and Taxes"

Zorko (a fat orange troll-thing) is
on this boss floor.  Make sure to look
for secret passages.  Without them you
will have a hard time defeating Zorko.

* If Zorko is killed, the board may
undergo some changes.  Take the first
first path you see after his defeat.
\
Entering Floor I, Volume II
"Hive"

The Silver Key is found in a secret
passage.  With it, you can unlock and
enter other secret passages, one of
which contains your first heat-seeker.

* Sometimes there will be a secret
passage within another secret passage.
You may view the full second episode
story under the STORY menu.
\
Entering Floor II, Volume II
"Door World"

Everywhere you turn in this floor
there will be a door within sight.

* You can easily get lost within this
maze of doors, unless you leave all
doors that you have been through open
so that you know where you've been.
\
Entering Floor III, Volume II
"Blue Bricks"

In the first main chamber, watch for
blue bricks.  They may indicate secret
passages.

* Do not attempt to destroy every enemy
in every room; sometimes it may not be
worth the risk.
\
Entering Floor IV, Volume II
"Another World"

Make sure to enter every building in
this floor, or you may miss something.

* Obey the warning signs when it comes
time to do unlocking.
\
Entering Floor V, Volume II
"Back and Forth"

You must be sure to search each
section over and over until you find
the stairs.

* Use warp zones to your advantage
because they will be necessary for
completion of the floor.
\
Entering Floor VI, Volume II
"Walkabout"

In the maze section there is a warp
very close to your entrance and one
very close to the key, so you don't
need to make your way through the maze.

* You may want to go to the section
full of pirates first to get food
available for when you need it.
\
Entering Floor VII, Volume II
"The Jolly Roger"

Watch out!  There are tons of pirate
Monsters in this floor, so load up
your six-shooter an' saddle-up your
horse to prepare the the shootin'est,
tootin'est ride this side o' the Pecos.

* Use strafe in the room with the many
empty key racks to find the silver key.
\
Entering Floor VIII, Volume II
"Off the Beaten Path"

This maze has TONS of pathways, and you
are sure to lose sight of the "beaten
path" somewhere in your travels.

* There are warps which you need to use
to beat the level.  Look for a warp
entrance and silver key behind gold
locked doors near the start.
\
Entering Floor IX, Volume II
"Long & Winding Road"

This floor starts you off in a maze.
South of the maze are some hallways and
rooms.

* shooting some paintings could cause
trouble later in your travels.
\
Entering Floor X, Volume II
"Way to Dusty Death"

This, the second boss floor, is home to
Andy and his Mini-Andy-Clones which you
encountered earlier.

* When Andy is invincible he can kill
you on contact and reflect your
bullets, so watch out!
\
Entering Floor I, Volume III
"Colosseum"

Try shooting walls in the Colosseum
(the area that this board gets its
name from) to discover a secret
passage.

* Search for the secret passage in the
colosseum area using sounds as clues.
You may view the full third episode
story under the STORY menu.
\
Entering Floor II, Volume III
"Halls of Confusion"

Look at the map and you will have
little trouble finding your way around
this floor.  In the western section,
there is a secret shortcut, and there
is a valuable item in the northwest.

* Remember to shoot the walls; some may
disintegrate to reveal shortcuts.
\
Entering Floor III, Volume III
"Seven Splits"

This floor is split up into seven
parts.  First find the silver key, then
search for the gold key to unlock the
door to the section with the stairs.

* In certain sections you should be
very careful; enemies like to come at
you from behind.
\
Entering Floor IV, Volume III
"Virtually real"

To find the first key, look for a
small building with four entrances
that has a mix of blue and red brick
walls.

* Don't spend too much time killing
enemies because they vastly outnumber
you.
\
Entering Floor V, Volume III
"Battleground"

Watch yourself; this area is very
confusing.  Make sure to search
everywhere.  Down any passage there
may be some hidden secret.

* Look very carefully at the map; you
may even want to copy it down to keep
from getting lost.
\
Entering Floor VI, Volume III
"Heartbeat"

This labyrinth is a tough one to
get past.  You need to have a good
fighting ability as well as a good
maze solving ability to get by alive.

* Remember that you can walk around
the edges of holes without falling.
\
Entering Floor VII, Volume III
"Power-up!"

Get the power-ups to prepare for the
challenge ahead.

* Sorry, no tips for this floor.  You
don't have to be a rocket scientist to
figure out what to do.
\
Entering Floor VIII, Volume III
"Hot on the Trail"

This maze is extremely confusing, but
clues will help you on your way.

* Think about the significance of the
title.  If at first glance, you don't
figure this one out, we recommend that
you schedule an appointment with an
optometrist.
\
Entering Floor IX, Volume III
"The Labyrinth"

In this floor, find your way through
the labyrinth before your time runs
out.  There is only one key, but it is
very hard to find.

* The exit is behind the locked doors
near your starting point.  Power-ups
are scattered throughout the floor.
\
Entering Floor X, Volume III
"This is it!"

So you say the first 29 were a breeze?
Well, let's see how you handle this
floor!

* There is no hidden map for this
level - you're on your own this time.
\
